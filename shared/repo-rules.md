## Repo rules

The repo rules are what the repo's agent files (`CLAUDE.md`, `AGENTS.md`) say, the files they point to that touch the task, and the Standard: `CODING_STANDARDS.md` at the repo root, with the `docs/standards/` files it links to. They come before the rules you were given. Where a repo rule and one of those rules differ, follow the repo rule, and follow a repo rule they lack too. Where the repo rules say nothing, the rules you were given hold.
